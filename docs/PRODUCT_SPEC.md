# OPERON — Product Specification (V1)

## Goal
Reduce operator memory burden and manual entry while preserving traceability.

## Core modules
1. Shift dashboard and handover
2. Equipment status/history
3. Logbook and free notes
4. Watch items
5. Timers and reminders
6. Procedures/checklists
7. Process-circuit knowledge
8. Photo capture and handwritten-note scan
9. Private/local AI assistant (later milestone)

## Input philosophy
Prefer 1–3 taps, short text, templates, equipment dictionaries, and photo/OCR over long forms.

## Safety boundary
AI may classify, retrieve, summarize, and draft. It must not independently assert safety-critical plant state, LOTO completion, PTW status, or procedural completion. Original operator records remain immutable/auditable.
